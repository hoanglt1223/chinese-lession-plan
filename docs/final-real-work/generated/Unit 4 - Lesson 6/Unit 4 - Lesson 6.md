| Stage & aim 教学环节与目标 | Activities ideas & Procedures 活动设计与教学步骤 | Materials / 教具 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Warm up 热身**<br>(Aim: To energize students and review fruit vocabulary in a fun, physical way.) | **Fruit Squat Game (水果蹲)**:<br>1. Give each student a fruit flashcard (苹果, 香蕉, 西瓜).<br>2. Students chant together while patting their knees: "苹果蹲，苹果蹲，苹果蹲完香蕉蹲" (Apple squats, apple squats, apple squats then banana squats).<br>3. The students holding the '香蕉' card immediately squat down.<br>4. Continue the chant, varying the fruit called. Speed up the pace to make it more exciting. | 水果闪卡 |
| **Rules 规则**<br>(Aim: Remind students of class rules for focused learning.) | 1. Use simple gestures to review rules: <br>   - Listen: Hand to ear. (耳朵听好)<br>   - Look: Point to eyes. (眼睛看好)<br>   - Hands up: Raise hand. (举手发言)<br>2. Praise students who are following the rules. | 手势 |
| **Review / Presentation**<br>(Aim: To review the target characters, their pinyin, and basic stroke order.) | 1. **Character Review**: Show the flashcard for 苹果. Ask "这是什么？" (What is this?). Students answer. Then focus on the character '苹'. Show the pinyin 'píng'.<br>2. **Stroke Review (复习笔画)**:<br>   a. Break down the character '苹' into its strokes on the whiteboard (e.g., 艹 is a vertical stroke (竖), two left-falling strokes (撇), etc.).<br>   b. Have students "air-write" the character with their fingers, following the teacher and saying the stroke names aloud.<br>3. Repeat the process for '香' (from 香蕉) and '西' (from 西瓜). | 汉字闪卡, 白板, 白板笔 |
| **Practice**<br>(Aim: To reinforce character recognition through interactive games.) | 1. **Sticky Ball Game (黏球大战)**:<br>   a. Stick the three large character cards (苹, 香, 西) on the board.<br>   b. Call out a fruit name, e.g., "xīguā!"<br>   c. A student comes to the front, finds the correct character '西', and throws the sticky ball at it.<br>2. **Little Detective (小侦探)**:<br>   a. Hand out a worksheet to each student. The worksheet has a jumble of many characters, including 苹, 香, and 西 hidden among others.<br>   b. Give each student a paper "magnifying glass."<br>   c. Call out a character, e.g., "请找到 苹！"<br>   d. Students use their magnifying glass to locate and circle the correct character on their sheet. | 黏球, 汉字闪卡, “小侦探”练习纸, 纸制放大镜, 彩笔 |
| **Production**<br>(Aim: To have students use the vocabulary and sentence pattern in a communicative context.) | **Market Role-Play Game (市场角色扮演)**:<br>1. Set up a "fruit stall" at the front of the class with the three fruit flashcards displayed.<br>2. One student is the "seller" (小老板). The other students are "customers" (顾客).<br>3. Each customer approaches the stall and says the sentence "我喜欢 [水果]." (e.g., "我喜欢香蕉。").<br>4. The seller listens, finds the correct fruit flashcard, and hands it to the customer, saying "给你！" (Here you are!).<br>5. Switch roles so multiple students can be the seller. | 水果闪卡 |
| **Wrap up 总结**<br>(Aim: To review key learning points and end the class positively.) | 1. **True or False Game (判断对错)**:<br>   a. Teacher holds up the flashcard for 西瓜.<br>   b. Teacher says a sentence, e.g., "这是苹果。".<br>   c. Students show a thumbs-down (错) or use a specified gesture for 'false'.<br>   d. Repeat with other correct/incorrect statements.<br>2. **Themed Goodbye Song**: Sing a simple goodbye song, substituting the fruit names. (To the tune of "Frère Jacques"):<br>   "再见再见, 苹果再见; 再见再见, 香蕉再见; 再见再见, 西瓜再见, 我们都再见, 大家再见。" | 水果闪卡 |
