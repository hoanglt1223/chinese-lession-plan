| Stage & aim 教学环节与目标 | Activities ideas & Procedures 活动设计与教学步骤 | Materials / 教具 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Warm up 热身**<br>(Aim: To get students engaged and ready for the lesson.) | **Activity: Number Chant (数字歌谣)**<br>1. Teacher shows number cards from 1 to 10.<br>2. Lead students to chant the numbers together, first slowly, then faster.<br>3. Use hand gestures to represent each number.<br>4. Repeat 2-3 times. | 数字卡片 |
| **Rules 规则**<br>(Aim: Remind class rules for smooth gameplay.) | 1. Teacher quickly reviews class rules: "Listen carefully" (认真听), "Raise your hand" (举手), "Take turns" (轮流).<br>2. Use a simple **Simon Says (老师说)** activity to practice listening. Teacher says "老师说，听一听" (Listen) and "老师说，看老师" (Look at the teacher). Students only do the action if "老师说" is said. | 无 |
| **Review / Presentation**<br>(Aim: To review vocabulary in an energetic, competitive way.) | **Activity: Flashcard Grabbing (抢卡片)**<br>1. Place vocabulary cards (e.g., colors) face up on the floor or table.<br>2. Divide students into two teams.<br>3. Teacher calls out a word (e.g., "红色").<br>4. One student from each team races to grab the correct card.<br>5. The team with the most cards wins. | 颜色词语卡片 |
| **Practice**<br>(Aim: To reinforce memory and visual recognition of vocabulary.) | **Activity: What's Missing? (什么不见了?)**<br>1. Place 3-5 vocabulary cards on the board.<br>2. Have students close their eyes.<br>3. Teacher removes one card and asks "什么不见了?" (What's missing?).<br>4. Students open their eyes and say the missing word. Increase difficulty by removing two cards. | 词语卡片 |
| **Production**<br>(Aim: To encourage students to actively use the language independently.) | **Activity: Matching Game (配对游戏)**<br>1. Prepare pairs of cards (e.g., a character card for "蓝色" and a picture card of something blue).<br>2. Place all cards face down on the floor.<br>3. Students take turns flipping over two cards, trying to find a matching pair.<br>4. When a pair is found, the student should say the word (e.g., "蓝色"). | 配对卡片 (汉字+图片) |
| **Wrap up 总结**<br>(Aim: To review and provide positive reinforcement.) | 1. Teacher quickly shows all the vocabulary cards from the lesson, having students chorus the words one last time.<br>2. Praise students for their good work.<br>3. Give out reward stickers.<br>4. Say goodbye (再见). | 词语卡片, 奖励贴纸 |
