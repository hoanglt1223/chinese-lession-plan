| **Warm up 热身**<br>(Aim: To get students' attention and create a positive learning atmosphere.) | 1. Greeting Song: Sing a "Hello Song" (你好歌) with actions.<br>2. Number Review: Quickly review numbers 1-5 using fingers and flashcards. | Music player for song, number flashcards (1-5). |
| **Rules 规则**<br>(Aim: Remind class rules for smooth lesson flow.) | Briefly remind students of class rules: "Listen carefully" (认真听), "Raise your hand" (举手), "Have fun" (玩得开心). Use simple gestures for each rule. | Rule flashcards with pictures. |
| **Presentation**<br>(Aim: To introduce new vocabulary in an engaging and memorable way.) | **Introduce Fruits with Mystery Bag:**<br>1. Show students the "Mystery Bag". Shake it and ask "What's inside?" (里面是什么？).<br>2. Let students come up, feel the bag, and guess in Chinese or English.<br>3. Take out one fruit (e.g., an apple). Show it and say clearly: "This is an apple, 苹果 ". Have students repeat 3-4 times.<br>4. Show the flashcard with the character 苹果. Point to the Pinyin and the character.<br>5. Repeat this process for 2-3 other fruits (e.g., 香蕉, 西瓜).<br>**Introduce Remaining Fruits with Flashcards:**<br>Use flashcards to quickly introduce the remaining fruits (梨, 橘子, 草莓, 葡萄) using the same "This is..." pattern. | A non-transparent bag, real fruits or fruit models (苹果, 香蕉, 西瓜), fruit flashcards (all 7 fruits). |
| **Practice**<br>(Aim: To reinforce vocabulary recognition and pronunciation through fun games.) | 1. **Fruit Squat (水果蹲):**<br>   - Give each student a fruit flashcard.<br>   - Teacher starts: "苹果蹲，苹果蹲，苹果蹲完，香蕉蹲" (Apple squats, apple squats, after apple squats, banana squats).<br>   - Students holding the "苹果" card squat. Then the "香蕉" students squat and continue the chant.<br>2. **Sticky Ball (黏球大战):**<br>   - Stick all fruit flashcards on the board.<br>   - Call out a fruit, e.g., "西瓜 ".<br>   - Students take turns throwing a sticky ball at the correct flashcard.<br>3. **Stroke Review (复习笔画) & Character Introduction:**<br>   - Introduce strokes 竖弯钩  and 横折弯钩 . Use arms to draw the strokes in the air.<br>   - Show how to write character 几  using 竖弯钩.<br>   - Show how to write character 西  using 横折弯钩.<br>   - Students practice writing in the air or on their palms. | Fruit flashcards (one per student), sticky balls, whiteboard, markers. |
| **Production**<br>(Aim: To encourage students to use the target language in a simple communicative task.) | **Fruit Market Role-play:**<br>1. Set up a "fruit stall" at the teacher's desk with the fruit models/flashcards.<br>2. Teacher acts as the seller: "你好！你要什么？" (Hello! What do you want?).<br>3. Model the response for students: "我要一个苹果 ." (I want one apple).<br>4. Invite students to come and "buy" fruit. Encourage them to use "我要 + 数字 + 个 + 水果". | Teacher's desk, fruit models/flashcards, prop money (optional). |
| **Wrap up 总结**<br>(Aim: To review the lesson content and end on a positive note.) | 1. **Quick Review:** Use flashcards for a final, fast review of all fruit names. Ask "这是什么？" (What is this?) and students answer.<br>2. **Goodbye Song:** Sing a "Goodbye Song" (再见歌) with actions.<br>3. **Praise:** Praise students for their good work and give out stickers. | All fruit flashcards, music player for song, stickers. |
