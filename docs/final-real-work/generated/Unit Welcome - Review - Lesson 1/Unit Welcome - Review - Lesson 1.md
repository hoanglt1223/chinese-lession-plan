# Lesson Plan: Unit Welcome / Review Lesson 1

## General Information
| **Unit** | Welcome / Review |
|---|---|
| **Lesson** | 1 |
| **Topic** | Unit Welcome / Review Lesson 1 |
| **Level** | Beginner |
| **Age Group** | Preschool |
| **Duration** | 45 mins |

## Objectives
- Học sinh có thể hiểu về sự xuất hiện của chữ Hán
- Học sinh làm quen với một số chữ tượng hình
- chữ Hội ý
- Học sinh hiểu được ý nghĩa của các hiệu lệnh trên lớp

## Vocabulary
- 汉字的出现
- 象形字
- 会意字
- 课堂用语

## Materials


## Procedure

| Stage | Time | Teacher Activity | Student Activity | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up** | 5 min | **Greeting & Song**<br>Teacher greets students with "Ni hao!". Play the "Hello Song". | Students wave and sing along. | Audio Player |
| **Presentation** | 10 min | **Flashcard Intro**<br>Show flashcards for: 汉字的出现, 象形字, 会意字, 课堂用语.<br>Pronounce each word clearly. | Listen and repeat after the teacher.<br>Point to the correct card when asked. | Flashcards |
| **Practice** | 15 min | **Game: Missing Card**<br>Place cards on board. Ask students to close eyes. Remove one. Ask "What's missing?" | Open eyes and shout out the missing word. | Flashcards, Board |
| **Production** | 10 min | **Role Play / Activity**<br>Students practice using the words in simple sentences or a group activity. | Work in pairs or groups. | Props |
| **Wrap-up** | 5 min | **Review & Goodbye**<br>Quick review of words. Sing "Goodbye Song". | Sing goodbye and wave. | Audio Player |

## Notes
- Ensure all students participate.
- Adjust pacing based on student engagement.
